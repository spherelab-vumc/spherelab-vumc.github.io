## Prospective students & postdocs

Lab members come from computer science, electrical and computer engineering, and biomedical informatics. Dr. Yin advises Ph.D. students through the **Department of Biomedical Informatics** at VUMC and the **Departments of Computer Science** and **Electrical and Computer Engineering** at Vanderbilt University.

If you are interested in trustworthy AI, clinical language, multimodal prediction, or computational approaches to disease and health-related behavior, please email Dr. Yin with:

1. your CV,
2. a short note on which [research direction](/research/) interests you and why, and
3. one piece of work you are proud of (a paper, a repository, or a project).

Vanderbilt undergraduates interested in research are also welcome to reach out.

> Please apply to a Vanderbilt Ph.D. program first; admissions are handled by the departments, not the lab.
