## Machine Learning and Natural Language Processing for Healthcare

*BMIF 6390 · CS 5891 · CS 3891 · Spring 2020–present*

This course explores the frontiers of machine learning, with a special focus on large language models and their potential to address real-world challenges in health and well-being. Students engage with influential peer-reviewed research from machine learning and biomedical informatics, gaining a deeper understanding of how emerging methods are shaping the future of healthcare. Topics include large language models and AI agents in medicine, multimodal and biomedical foundation models, learning health behaviors and outcomes from patient-generated data, causal inference, and evaluating and supervising AI for reliable use in care. Although a variety of machine learning models are discussed, the course is designed for graduate and advanced undergraduate students with prior experience in the field. Participants are expected to read and critically evaluate research papers, write reviews, and complete course projects.
