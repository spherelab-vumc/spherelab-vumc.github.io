questions:
  - "Which genes, proteins, and cell types drive colorectal adenoma and cancer risk?"

In collaboration with Dr. Xingyi Guo's group, this direction integrates large-scale genetic and omics data with longitudinal health records to nominate risk genes and proteins and evaluate drug-repurposing candidates.
