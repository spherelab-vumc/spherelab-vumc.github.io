With Dr. Susannah Rose, the lab co-leads the ARPA-H Vanderbilt Chatbot Accuracy and Reliability Evaluation System (V-CARES), which detects hallucinations, omissions, and misaligned responses from LLMs in healthcare. Related work covers confidence-aware reasoning, knowledge-graph-grounded benchmarks, and evidence-aware alignment for clinical summarization.
