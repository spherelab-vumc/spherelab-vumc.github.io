Many patients with breast cancer stop adjuvant hormonal therapy early, often after describing side effects in their own words. Since 2017 this direction has followed that signal from online breast-cancer forums to patient portal messages and structured EHR data, and most recently to fine-tuned language models whose predictions remain interpretable. It is supported by an NCI MERIT Award.
