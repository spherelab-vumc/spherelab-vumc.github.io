summary: "Making large language, vision-language, and speech models reliable enough for clinical use: grounded in evidence, calibrated, and robust outside the lab."

Foundation models now read clinical notes, answer patients, interpret images, and listen to speech. This program measures where they fail and builds methods that make their behavior verifiable.
