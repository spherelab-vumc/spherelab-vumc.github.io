title: "Learning from Human Interactions in Health"
short: "Human interactions"
summary: "Computational analysis of how patients, caregivers, and clinicians communicate, in portal messages, online communities, and clinical records, to understand and predict health behavior."
---
People describe side effects, worries, and needs in their own words long before these appear in structured data. This program turns those interactions into signals that clinicians and researchers can act on.
