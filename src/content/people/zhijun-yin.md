Zhijun Yin is a tenured Associate Professor of Biomedical Informatics at Vanderbilt University Medical Center, with secondary appointments in Computer Science and Electrical and Computer Engineering. Dr. Yin develops computational and multimodal methods that turn heterogeneous health data, from patient interactions and clinical records to speech, images, and omics, into discoveries, predictions, and trustworthy AI for healthcare, with particular emphasis on supervisory AI.

Dr. Yin directs the SPHERE Lab, leading and co-leading more than $14M in research funded by the National Cancer Institute, ARPA-H, the American Heart Association, and other sponsors. Dr. Yin is AI Foundation Lead of ADVANCE at Vanderbilt, Co-Founder and AI Lead of the national Cleft AI-VPD Collaborative, and an Associate Editor for *JMIR* and *JMIR AI*.
