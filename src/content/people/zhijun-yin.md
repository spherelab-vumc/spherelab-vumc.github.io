Zhijun Yin is a tenured Associate Professor of Biomedical Informatics at Vanderbilt University Medical Center, with secondary appointments in Computer Science and Electrical and Computer Engineering at Vanderbilt University. Trained in computer science and biostatistics, Dr. Yin develops computational methods and multimodal approaches that turn heterogeneous biomedical and health data, from patient and caregiver interactions to clinical records, speech, images, and omics, into discoveries, predictions of health behaviors and outcomes, and trustworthy intelligence for healthcare, with particular emphasis on supervisory AI.

Dr. Yin directs the SPHERE Lab, leading and co-leading more than $14M in research funded by the National Cancer Institute, ARPA-H, the American Heart Association, and other sponsors. Dr. Yin is a Core Founding Member and AI Foundation Lead of ADVANCE (AI Discovery & Vigilance to Accelerate iNnovation and Clinical Excellence) at Vanderbilt and Co-Founder and AI Lead of the Cleft AI-VPD Collaborative, a national, multi-institutional collaborative of speech-language pathologists, surgeons, and data scientists. Dr. Yin also contributes to major NIH initiatives including AIM-AHEAD and Bridge2AI, serves as an Associate Editor for *JMIR* and *JMIR AI*, and serves on NIH and PCORI review panels. During doctoral training, Dr. Yin was a research intern at HP Labs (2015) and the IBM Thomas J. Watson Research Center (2016 and 2017).
